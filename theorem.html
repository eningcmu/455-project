Lecture 2 · Section 06 of 08

The 3k-vertex kernel theorem

35 minutesPlanned content

What this page will cover

This section will prove that Vertex Cover admits polynomial-time preprocessing that either correctly rejects the instance or produces an equivalent instance with at most 3k vertices, where k is the original budget. The central step will be a constructive crown-finding lemma, developed using matchings and alternating paths rather than stated without proof. The section will then assemble the repeated-reduction algorithm and prove its correctness, polynomial running time, and final size bound, with explicit handling of terminal cases.

Proof plan: find a crown or a rejection certificate → apply a safe reduction → bound the remaining graph.
