What this page will cover

This section will present a complete worked example from the original graph through successive reductions, tracking the remaining budget and reconstructing an original cover from a reduced solution. Static graph diagrams will show each stage of the reduction. The lecture will conclude by reviewing the roles of branching, safe preprocessing, and matching structure in the quadratic and 3k-vertex kernel proofs.
