Lecture 2 · Section 05 of 08

Matchings and crown reductions

30 minutesPlanned content

What this page will cover

This section will introduce the matching background needed for the main result, including maximal versus maximum matchings, matching-based lower bounds, and alternating paths. It will define a crown decomposition using an independent set, its neighborhood, and a matching saturating that neighborhood. A labeled example and an exchange argument will support a full proof that the crown reduction preserves the answer in both directions, including how to lift a reduced solution.
