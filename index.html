Parameterized Complexity and Vertex Cover

15-455 project: fixed-parameter algorithms, kernelization, and the 3k-vertex kernel theorem.

Start with section 1: Motivation

About these notes

Project scope

These notes introduce parameterized complexity through Vertex Cover. They assume familiarity with graphs, asymptotic running time, and NP-hardness, and introduce the matching concepts needed for the crown argument. The material moves from fixed-parameter algorithms and basic preprocessing to crown reductions and a kernelization theorem with a 3k-vertex bound.

The notes develop formal definitions, a branching algorithm, safe preprocessing rules, a quadratic kernel, crown reductions, and the 3k-vertex kernel theorem.
