Why parameterized complexity?

10 minutesPlanned content

What this page will cover

This section will motivate studying NP-hard problems through a second measure of input difficulty: a parameter k. It will introduce Vertex Cover, compare two small graph instances with the same cover budget, and set up the question of what becomes possible when k is small.
