Lecture 1 · Section 04 of 08

Kernelization and a quadratic kernel

30 minutesPlanned content

What this page will cover

This section will define kernelization, emphasizing polynomial preprocessing time, preservation of yes/no answers, and a size bound depending only on the parameter. It will prove the isolated-vertex and high-degree rules safe in both directions, derive the edge-count rejection test and an O(k2)-vertex bound, and account for updates to the budget. A worked reduction sequence will then connect the kernel to the branching algorithm and show how to recover a cover of the original graph.
